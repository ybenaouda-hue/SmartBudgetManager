## What it does
SmartBudget Manager is an Excel workbook that helps users record monthly income and expenses, organize expenses by category, review their monthly balance, and monitor spending against a budget. The Dashboard includes a summary and an expense-by-category pie chart.

## What you need
- Microsoft Excel 2016 or later, Microsoft 365, or a compatible spreadsheet application.
- The file `SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx`.
- Basic knowledge of opening and editing a spreadsheet.

## Worksheets
- **Dashboard:** Shows income, expenses, monthly balance, budget status, category summary, and chart.
- **Transactions:** Used to enter each income or expense.
- **Categories:** Lists expense categories and their monthly budget amounts. The overall budget is calculated from these category budgets.

## Getting started
1. Open `SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx`.
2. Select **Transactions** and enter each transaction on a new row.
3. Enter the date, description, type, category, and amount in the correct column. Use either Income or Expense for each transaction.
4. Review **Dashboard** to check the totals and chart.
5. If needed, review the category budget amounts on **Categories**.

## Important note
The workbook includes example transactions. Review or replace them with your own data before using the totals. Do not overwrite the automatically calculated **Month** column. Keep a backup copy of your file.
